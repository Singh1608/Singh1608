REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Title: Design, Supply, Installation and 5-Year Operation of an Integrated Traffic Management System
Tender No: MTA/ITMS/2026/042
Issued by: Metropolitan Transport Authority
Sector: Smart City / Transport Technology
Location: Northern Region

1. Introduction
The Metropolitan Transport Authority invites sealed proposals from eligible bidders for the design, supply, installation, commissioning and five-year operation and maintenance of an Integrated Traffic Management System covering 180 signalised junctions. The system shall include adaptive signal control, automatic number plate recognition and a central command centre.

2. Key Information
Estimated contract value: USD 12.5 million
Bid security (EMD): USD 250,000 in the form of a bank guarantee
Contract period: 18 months implementation followed by 60 months O&M; contract duration 78 months
Pre-bid meeting: {PREBID}
Last date for submission of bids: {DEADLINE}
Bid opening: {OPENING}

3. Scope of Work
- Supply and installation of adaptive traffic signal controllers at 180 junctions
- ANPR and red-light violation detection cameras at 60 locations
- Central Integrated Command and Control Centre with video wall
- Fibre-optic network connectivity and data centre hosting
- Five years of comprehensive operation and maintenance with 99.5% uptime SLA
- Training of 120 authority staff

4. Eligibility Criteria
4.1 The bidder shall have an average annual turnover of not less than USD 20 million over the last three financial years.
4.2 Net worth of at least USD 5 million as of the last audited balance sheet.
4.3 The bidder shall have at least 7 years of experience in intelligent transport systems.
4.4 At least one similar project of value not less than USD 6 million completed in the last seven years.
4.5 The bidder must hold valid ISO 9001:2015, ISO 27001:2022 and CMMI Level 3 certifications.

5. Evaluation
Proposals will be evaluated on Quality and Cost Based Selection (QCBS) with 70:30 technical:financial weightage. Minimum technical score to qualify is 70.

6. Commercial Terms
6.1 Performance bank guarantee of 10% of contract value valid until 90 days after the O&M period.
6.2 Mobilisation advance of 10% against bank guarantee.
6.3 Payment will be released within 90 days of acceptance of milestone invoices.
6.4 Retention of 5% of each running bill until completion of the defect liability period.
6.5 Liquidated damages at 0.5% per week of delay, subject to a maximum of 10% of contract value.
6.6 The contract price is a fixed price and shall not be subject to any escalation or price variation.

7. Legal
7.1 The contractor shall indemnify the Authority against any and all losses, including indirect and consequential losses, arising from the performance of the contract.
7.2 The Authority may terminate the contract for its convenience with 30 days' notice.
7.3 All intellectual property in software developed under this contract shall vest in the Authority.
7.4 Non-performance may lead to blacklisting of the contractor for three years.
